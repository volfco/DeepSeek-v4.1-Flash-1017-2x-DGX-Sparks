**Knob semantics.** `TF_DS_DRAFT_MS` unset/"" keeps 3.5 (byte-identical default); a float (e.g.
`7.2`) overrides; `0`, negatives, `nan`, `inf`, garbage → `error.BadDraftMs`, refused at startup.
The measured drafter pass is **3.75–3.90 ms at one stream, ~7.2 at four, ~11 at eight**
(`runs/trace-review-20261123/out/pass_x4.txt`, trace-review RESULTS §5), so the operator sets it at
the served stream count; a per-stream-count cost model is a follow-up, not this knob.

**Clamp fix (chose "fix" over "document"; the recovery follow-up named it "clamp fix").**
Old: `rows = @min(round_ms.len, others + kk + 1)` — every round wider than 24 rows priced at
`round_ms[23]` = 109.2 ms **regardless of k**, so the marginal cost of another draft read zero and
`chooseK` pinned `k = kmax` whenever clamped. New: rows past the table price at the tail slope
(`round_ms[23] - round_ms[22]` = 3.1 ms/row). Why this is safe without its own switch: `chooseK`
has **no production caller** (it is the `multi.py` rule's port used by the replay tools
`layer_main.zig` to check recorded windows) and the clamp is unreachable at the served 16-row
rounds — `depth(8, ·, 16) = 1` caps priced rows at 20. It is reachable under wider rounds
(`TF_DS_ROUND_ROWS >= 24`, e.g. the four-node lane's 48) or deeper pins: 8 streams at 48-row rounds
price 29 + k rows. The served policy's own row-cost clamp (`lanes.zig` `Lanes.init`, `wc`/`sc` at
`t[@min(i, t.len - 1)]`) is width-bounded (window rows ≤ 17) and does not fire; left alone.

## Open decisions (for the operator / parent)

1. **Wall-calibrated table vs the shipped forward.** The table's contract is forward (shipped); the
   policy may price better on wall. A wall-calibrated variant is `fwd + 8.2 ms` (4-stream anchor) or
   `fwd + 25.5 ms` (8-stream anchor) — the two anchors disagree by 17.3 ms, which is the stream
   term. M1's win came from pricing wide rounds *dearer*; the 8-stream calibration is the dearer
   one. Operator's call; the script prints everything needed (`--variant` covers arm choice).
2. **The stream-count term** (the item's third defect): impossible in a rows-only table. The
   designed home is `depth.zig` `Rule.overhead` ("by streams"); feeding it from the walls
   (+25.5 ms @ 8, +8.2 ms @ 4) is a separate item behind its own switch.
3. **Replay parity under a non-default knob.** `layer_main`'s `window_ok` compares `chooseK`
   against recordings priced at 3.5 ms / the served table; under `TF_DS_DRAFT_MS` ≠ 3.5 the kept
   window may move and that check will flag it. Replay-parity gates run at the default (unchanged);
   this is expected behavior, recorded so it is not read as a regression.
