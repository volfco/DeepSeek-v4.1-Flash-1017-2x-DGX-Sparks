# Scheduling the two-Spark decode gathers

**First measure both directions of the same exchange.** Across 904 rounds at 7.22 rows/round, rank 0 reported about 4 µs in staging, 22 µs from its doorbell to observing the receive flag, and 3 µs in copy-out. The 76% is a ratio of rounded aggregate means over the probed exchanges. The report contains neither per-layer distributions nor the corresponding rank 1 observations. Rank skew is a plausible contributor whose magnitude remains open. [Raw rank 0 profile](probe1.profile.txt) · [Run and settings](probe1.armed.txt)

The publishing stage block timestamps just after ringing the doorbell. The collecting block timestamps after launch, PDL entry, polling the peer flag and a block barrier. In between, the peer must have produced and staged its partial, its CPU proxy must observe its doorbell and issue RDMA writes, and the receiving proxy must poll completions and publish the flag after all configured NIC parts arrive. Local copy of the sender's own slice can already overlap this path. The aggregate mixes hidden-state gathers with other probed ring operations. Its means cannot assign a particular exchange to one of those causes. ## Smallest experiment that separates the causes

1. **Measure the protocol with back-to-back gathers.** During an exclusive two-board benchmark window, use the existing graph-replay RDMA bench with width 5,120 and rows 1, 7 and the modal four-stream row count from the matched run. Match production's NICs, GID, PDL, proxy CPU mask and ring settings explicitly (the standalone creator omits proxy pinning and uses its own GID default). Keep both ranks' byte checks, repeat at each size, and measure the probe-off control. This prices the ring with little intervening computation and reveals the size-dependent slope. [Existing benchmark](/home/colum/Workspace/tfref-fnext-rebase/zig/src/families/dsv41/rdma_bench_main.zig:64) · [Served ring setup](/home/colum/Workspace/tfref-fnext-rebase/zig/src/families/dsv41/model.zig:930)
2. **Sample matched graph-on decode rounds.** Use a bounded per-rank trace buffer, keyed by the live ring sequence on replay, round, layer, operation, rows and actual bytes. For each sampled sequence record local GPU producer completion, doorbell publication, collect entry after PDL, flag observation and copy completion. In preallocated host buffers record proxy doorbell observed, last write posted, each NIC's receive completion observed and flag publication. Track drop counts. Drain outside the timed window. Separate hidden attention/MoE, KV, Engram and head traffic, idle wakeups, graph misses and steady replays. Probe both ranks at the same sampling interval. [Ring sequence and probes](/home/colum/Workspace/tfref-fnext-rebase/zig/src/families/dsv41/rdma.zig:724)
3. **Use same-clock differences.** For each role, compare GPU time from the previous gather's completion to this doorbell, time from collect entry to observing the flag, and local CPU proxy durations. The prior gather supplies a recurring alignment landmark with some residual uncertainty. Pair by sequence and compare with the standalone distribution. A growing producer interval on one rank paired with excess wait on the other implicates the producer. Long doorbell-to-post or completion-to-flag intervals implicate proxy service. Long similar waits on both sides, also seen at the same bytes in the standalone bench, implicate the protocol floor. Quote p50/p95 per operation and rank. Absolute one-way timing across hosts or between a GPU and its proxy additionally requires calibrated clock offsets and uncertainties below the size of the effect.

The attention gather feeds the FFN mix and expert routing. The MoE gather feeds the following residual mix. Their serial dependency rules out simply submitting both together or running the next layer's forward early. The HC side stream already handles independent Sinkhorn work. Any staged send/collect split must preserve the current ring sequence, slot-release checks, ordering with KV exchanges, and graph-capture joins. Adding a barrier can lower one rank's measured gather wait by moving the wait upstream. Judge each candidate on probe-off graphs-on forward ms, sampled output tok/s and p95 inter-token latency at one and four streams, with the exact panel, seeded identity and matched round/row counts.
